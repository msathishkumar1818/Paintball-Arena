Rules of Engagement & Digital Clearance

Safety is our absolute operational mandate. Clear these 4 foundational safety rules and submit your electronic waiver to fast-track your arena deployment.

MANDATORY RULES OF ENGAGEMENT STRICT ENFORCEMENT
ROE-01 MANDATORY

Masks Locked On Field

Never lift or loosen goggles inside netting or combat sectors for any reason. Immediate ejection upon breach.

ROE-02 STAGING SAFE

Barrel Socks In Safe Zone

Safety catches engaged and high-visibility barrel blocking socks secured at all times outside active battle zones.

ROE-03 RADAR CHRONO

280 FPS Velocity Ceiling

Every marker is clocked through the radar chronograph. Over-velocity hardware will not be cleared for play.

Within 10 feet of an unsuspecting opponent, offer the verbal "Surrender" command to prevent point-blank hits.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about deposits, cancellations, bad weather, and age requirements.

The minimum age for regular .68 caliber tactical paintball is 10 years old. Operators aged 10-17 must have a digital waiver signed by a parent or legal guardian prior to field entry.
We play rain or shine! In fact, light rain creates intense, atmospheric combat. If extreme weather occurs, you can transfer your session to our covered all-weather AstroTurf Speedball stadium or reschedule free of charge.
Paintball Arena is strictly Field Paint Only (FPO) to ensure biodegradable, non-toxic, and non-staining quality. You are welcome to bring your own personal markers and masks, provided they pass our 280 FPS chronograph and ASTM safety inspection.
Bookings can be rescheduled at no extra charge up to 48 hours before your session time. Cancellations made at least 72 hours in advance receive a 100% full refund to the original payment method.
