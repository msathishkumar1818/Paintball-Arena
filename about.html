The Origin Story

From One Field to a Tactical Empire

In 2011, three ex-army veterans and two paintball obsessives pooled their savings to transform a plot of land outside the metro into something the region had never seen — a real combat arena, not a muddy backyard.

2011 — Launch
Opened with 2 acres, 3 zones, and 40 rental markers.
2015 — Expansion
Added speedball tournament grid and electronic hit scoring system.
2019 — Night Ops
Launched night operations with UV floodlights and thermal masks.
2023 — 14 Acres
Expanded to full 14-acre warzone with 6 distinct battlefield theaters.
2026 — #1 Arena
Ranked region's top tactical paintball facility for the 3rd consecutive year.
